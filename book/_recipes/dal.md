* `a handful of dal`

* `water`

* `a little salt`

* `a pinch of hing`

* `two onions`

> Take a handful of dal, wash it well

---

> Add water, a little salt, and a pinch of hing

---

> Chop two onions

---

> Cook the onions slow till they turn soft and brown

---

> Add the dal to the onion and let it simmer

---

> Tips: Don't rush the onion. That is the whole recipe, and that is the secret also.
